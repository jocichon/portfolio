Mirror or Self-Portrait: A Systematic Review of AI Ethics Literature

An ongoing systematic review, with Dr. Brianna Dym, of how AI ethics literature constructs ethical meaning. From 219 screened ACM papers, 62 were coded by how their language expresses ethics, producing an emergent two-axis framework: AI as mirror vs. self-portrait, and ethics as checklist vs. situated. Grounded in existentialism, the framework treats AI systems as expressions of their creators' decisions. Next phases: a labeled dataset and an ML classifier to automate classification across the AI lifecycle.

Presented at the Khoury Spring MS CS Showcase, April 2026.
