Who I am

I'm Jo Cichon, a software engineer and AI ethics researcher who also leads teams as a project manager. I started in software development at Green River College and went on to graduate study in computer science at Northeastern University's Khoury College of Computer Sciences.

I build applications that put people first, from a case management tool for Indigenous student support to an AI assistant for nurses, and I study how the people who build AI systems shape the ethics those systems carry.

Research

My current research, Mirror or Self-Portrait, is a systematic review of AI ethics literature with Dr. Brianna Dym. It examines how papers construct ethical meaning and proposes a framework for reading AI systems as expressions of their creators' decisions.

Writing

I write about technology and AI ethics on Medium (opens in a new tab). My first article is up, with more on the way.
